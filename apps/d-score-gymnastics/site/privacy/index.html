PRIVACY POLICY

Your routines stay yours.

Effective August 22, 2026 · Version 1.0

Overview

D-Score Gymnastics is published and operated by Jose Gabriel Cintron. The app is designed to work without an account and does not collect, upload, sell, or share routine data.

Information the app collects

The app does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting services, tracking technologies, or account registration. It does not request contact, location, health, photo, microphone, or fitness data.

Local application data

Entered routines, saved all-arounds, preferences, and editor state are stored locally in IndexedDB on the web or SQLite in the native app. This information remains under the user's control and is not transmitted by D-Score Gymnastics.

Backups and deletion

Backup files are created only when the user asks to export one. The user chooses where that file is saved or shared. Settings includes an option to permanently clear all local app data. Removing browser storage or uninstalling the app may also delete local data.

Website delivery

The public website is delivered by Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosting providers, Cloudflare may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details, and timestamps under its own privacy terms. D-Score Gymnastics does not receive an analytics dashboard or combine that information with routine data.

Contact and external links

If a user emails support or opens a GitHub issue, the information they choose to provide is handled by their email provider or GitHub under those services' terms. Public issues should not contain private routine or personal information.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience sports utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because there is no account or cloud service, the operator cannot identify individual users through the app.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Features that introduce data collection will not be added without updating the policy and the applicable store disclosures first.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@d-score.app.
