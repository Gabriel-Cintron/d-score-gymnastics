ABOUT THE PROJECT

Scoring logic you can inspect.

D-Score Gymnastics is a free, open-source, local-first routine planning tool. Version 1.0 supports senior men's artistic gymnastics for the 2025–2028 cycle.

What it calculates

The app calculates recognized-as-entered difficulty from the skills and relationships selected by the user. It covers all six MAG events, including apparatus-specific element groups, repetition limits, connections, Pommel Horse sequences, landing bonus, and Vault difficulty.

What it does not calculate

It does not judge execution, technical recognition from video, neutral deductions, or produce an official competition result. Coaches, athletes, and judges should resolve competition questions against the current governing publications.

Private by design

Routines and preferences remain in local browser or app storage. There is no account, advertising, analytics, or cloud synchronization. Versioned backups let users move or protect their own work.
